Project introduction

I led the redesign and launch of AHSNB’s website, focusing on three priorities: clearer navigation, consistent visual design, and more manageable content.

Starting with ambiguous requirements, limited time, and limited resources, I worked closely with my supervisor and stakeholders to clarify priorities and guide the design process. I reorganized the information architecture, established reusable visual patterns, and divided dense content into clearer pages and sections.

When unexpected database and hosting issues threatened the launch, I took ownership of coordinating troubleshooting with the hosting provider and restoring the site. The project expanded the original 11-page website into a 17-page responsive experience designed to make community information easier to find and explore.

Core design priorities

01 — Clearer Navigation

Reorganized the site structure and navigation to make key information easier to find.

02 — Visual Consistency

Established consistent typography, colour, spacing, and reusable UI patterns across the website.

03 — More Manageable Content

Restructured dense organizational content into focused pages and scannable sections.
